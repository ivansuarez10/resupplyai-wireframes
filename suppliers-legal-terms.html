Supplier Terms of Service

Last updated: June 18, 2026 · This is a draft for partner review, not final legal language.

1. What ResupplyAI does

ResupplyAI matches your inventory against open DLA solicitations and forwards your quotes to buyers. We act as an intermediary; we do not buy your goods ourselves. Using the supplier platform is free.

2. Your account

Accounts are tied to a company. You're responsible for keeping your credentials and your team's logins secure, and for the accuracy of the inventory, pricing, and certifications you provide. Each teammate must use their own login.

3. Quotes & pricing

  • You set your own prices. ResupplyAI never sets or suggests a price on your behalf.
  • Quotes must cover the full quantity requested — partial quotes aren't accepted.
  • A quote is an offer to sell at the stated price and terms; you're expected to honor it if selected.

4. Awards, purchase orders & fulfillment

If a buyer is awarded a solicitation that includes your quote, ResupplyAI shares the billing details so you can issue your purchase order. Once your PO is uploaded, the sale, shipping, invoicing, and payment are handled directly between you and the buyer. ResupplyAI is not a party to that transaction.

5. Data & confidentiality

You retain ownership of your inventory and pricing data. Suppliers never see other suppliers, and you never see the buyer until an award. We use your data only to operate the matching service. See our Privacy Policy.

6. Acceptable use

Don't scrape, reverse-engineer, overload, or attempt to access data that isn't yours. Automated access outside provided features is prohibited. We may suspend accounts that abuse the platform.

7. Disclaimers & changes

The service is provided "as is." We may update these terms; we'll notify the account's primary email of material changes. Continued use means acceptance.
