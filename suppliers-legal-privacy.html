Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026 · This is a draft for partner review, not final legal language.

1. What we collect

  • Account info — company name, CAGE, contact name, business email, team logins.
  • Inventory & quotes — the stock you upload (NSN, P/N, quantity, price, condition, certifications) and the quotes you submit.
  • Usage — logins, device/IP, and activity needed to run and secure the service.

2. How we use it

To match your inventory to DLA solicitations, forward your quotes to buyers, notify you of trace requests and wins, and keep the platform secure. We never sell your data, and we never share your pricing with other suppliers.

3. Who can see what

Suppliers never see other suppliers. You don't see the buyer until an award, and the buyer doesn't see you until then. Only ResupplyAI's internal operations team can see across both sides, and only to run the matching service.

4. Storage & security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is restricted to your company account. We apply reasonable safeguards — access controls, audit logging, and validation of uploaded files. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

5. Retention & your choices

We keep your data while your account is active. You can update or remove inventory anytime, disconnect your data source, or request account deletion by contacting us. Notification preferences are managed in Settings.

6. Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed to keep you signed in and secure the session. We don't use third-party advertising trackers.
