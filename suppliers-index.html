Role-based sign-in

From the login, a @resupplyai.com email enters the Ops console above; any other email lands on the supplier daily matches. One door, routed by role.

How matching works behind the scenes

Each DLA solicitation is cross-referenced against every supplier's stock, first by NSN (exact), then by approved Part Number + manufacturer, then by AI for the messy cases. Suppliers only ever see their own matches.
