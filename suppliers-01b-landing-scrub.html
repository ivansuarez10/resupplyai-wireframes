Questions suppliers ask.

Is ResupplyAI really free?

Yes, no subscription and no listing fees. We only make money when a buyer wins an award with your quote and purchases from you.

Do you see or share my pricing?

Your pricing stays private. You quote to ResupplyAI and we assign it to a buyer. Your competitors never see your numbers, and there's no open bidding war.

Who are the buyers?

Companies actively bidding on DLA solicitations. We route your quote to one that fits, and you only sell if they win the award.

What about export-controlled (ITAR/EAR) parts?

At every upload you confirm your files contain no export-controlled or otherwise restricted material. Only share what you're authorized to share through the platform.

How fast will I see matches?

Upload your inventory today and see your first matches tomorrow morning, refreshed every day against the latest solicitations.

Do I have to quote everything?

No. Quote the matches you want and skip the rest. Most suppliers spend just a few minutes a day in the queue.
